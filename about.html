I'm a fourth-year Electronics, Communication & Information Engineering student at Kathmandu Engineering College, IOE, Tribhuvan University — working at the intersection of embedded systems, applied machine learning, and the kind of robotics that actually has to run outdoors.

I'm a Fuse AI Fellow (Fusemachines, 2026 cohort), having built an agentic Text-to-SQL pipeline, tree-based ensemble models with SHAP explainability, Bayesian inference workflows in PyMC, and a full customer-segmentation pipeline (K-Means/Hierarchical/DBSCAN) on the UCI Online Retail II dataset. My capstone, BiasAperture, is a vision-model fairness & bias audit tool — a diagnostic layer that flags demographic performance disparities in deployed classifiers without retraining them. In parallel, I'm an NSSR DataCamp Fellow (Cohort 2), progressing through SQL Associate and Python Data Associate certification tracks after completing six applied GenAI projects in Phase 1.

For my BEI major project, SPARK, I lead the embedded & ML architecture within our four-person team — engineering a two-layer, fully on-device fall-detection wearable with an explainable gateway pipeline for eldercare (an MPU6050 IMU feeding a threshold gate and a TFLite Micro CNN on-device, paired with SHAP-attributed alerts and clinical-format incident reports at a Raspberry Pi 4B gateway). Beyond the bench, I serve as Vice Chair of the IEEE KEC KTM Student Branch and Event Manager at the Electronics Project Club, and volunteer as a Makerspace Ambassador running the club's web infrastructure.

Rather than accumulating disconnected prototypes, my work follows an integrated vertical systems trajectory — bridging physical IMU kinematics (SPARK) and vision bias auditing (BiasAperture) to Win32 NT kernel memory optimization (NovaOptimizer) and multi-account FastMCP orchestration (Super-NLM / Brainstorm). Every technical claim in my ecosystem adheres to strict E0–E5 epistemic evidence tiers (separating formal specifications, SimPy simulations, and physically measured hardware benchmarks). I treat AI not as an unexamined shortcut, but as an aggressive engineering force multiplier while continuously deepening underlying computer architecture, mathematical, and systems engineering foundations.

Epistemic Standard · E0 to E5

Evidence-Driven Verification

Every technical claim in this ecosystem is strictly segregated by verification tier to ensure epistemic honesty and prevent abstraction drift:

Tier E4 · Experimentally Verified Physical hardware or benchmark execution with empirical metrics (SPARK on-device 0.9185 SisFall AUC-ROC; BiasAperture BCa bootstrap CIs).
Tier E3 · Implemented & Simulation Committed code with unit tests, or discrete-event queueing models (STRANGLER-IPU 4.12× latency reduction via SimPy). Never presented as physical silicon.
Tier E0/E1 · Specification & Proposed Formal interface contracts and neurosymbolic schemas awaiting empirical test (Headless Invariant Assurance via Z3 SMT).
Operational Autonomy Metric Evaluated against the Walk-Away Benchmark and Autonomy Ratio (Compute Time / Human Intervention Minutes) to measure true independence from human babysitting.

Simulation results are never conflated with physical measurements.
